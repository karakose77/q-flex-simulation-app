Physics Behind the Simulation

This simulation is based on a 2nd-order Mass-Spring-Damper model representing the sensor's suspension system.

  • Equation of Motion: The system's frequency response is calculated via its transfer function: H(s) = 1 / (m*s^2 + c*s + k)
  • Impact of Stiffness: Increasing the spring stiffness (k) shifts the system's resonant frequency toward higher frequencies.
  • Cut-off Frequency: The -3dB point on the graph marks the effective bandwidth limit.
